💌 Letter for You 💌

December 19, 2024

I just had the urge to write how much I love my girlfriend. This feeling hit me when I looked at her picture, the one where she was wearing her Hello Kitty pajamas and laced sleeveless black top.

I stared at the photo, admiring her eyes. Her eyes, shaped like a cat’s, pull me in with just a glance. Her stare is so leading, so captivating. And that smile—so beautiful. Her lips, soft and full, look so perfect. The kind that makes me want to kiss her forever.

I don’t always know how to put my feelings into words, but one thing I know for sure—I love her. So much. And I’d do anything to make her happy and safe.

Looking at that picture reminded me of something—damn, I really pulled my dream girl. I get to stare at her and say, “That’s my girlfriend.” 🥹 Now I understand why I pulled every crazy stunt to be with her— my heart, my soul, everything in me just knew she was the one.
